Everything on this board is simulated. Turnout codes and known appliance IDs come from this repo's Store/ lists; other appliance IDs are placeholders. Station positions are approximate. Appliance status codes (responding, at incident, returning, on station) are made up for the simulation: the real meaning of the status part of appliance messages is unknown.

Every call is synthesised as audio (20 ms tones on the CCIR frequencies), noise is added for the chosen signal strength, and the board only shows what the decoder recovers. Coastline: GSHHS full resolution.
