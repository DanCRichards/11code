Turnout Board — design options

The same screens drawn several ways: the Live view on a phone, a My board tile wall as it would look on a TV, the full-screen turnout alarm, and each look's colours and type. The data is the same in every one so you can compare like for like.

Status colours keep the same meaning throughout: turned out, responding, at incident, out of area, available, on station.

HOW IT WORKS

  • Every truck is one row. The thin strip on the left and the K code chip carry the status colour; the rest stays calm, so the one red row stands out.
  • "In status" counts up, so you can see 205 has been responding for 19 seconds and 255 has sat on station for over an hour. Responding times turn amber.
  • Jobs are on the left with their units and the first-arrival code (K99, K77). The radio log runs down the right, and priority calls get a yellow row.
  • A turnout shows as a red alarm bar along the bottom instead of covering the screen. It still sounds and un-mutes the radio, and F1 acknowledges it.
  • On a phone (right) it shrinks to the units table alone, still grouped by station.

Good at

  • Shows the most trucks per screen, and the whole region at once
  • Time in status and the radio log at a glance
  • Keyboard-driven, feels like the real thing

Watch out for

  • Dense: hard to read from across a room
  • Needs a wide screen; on a phone it's a list
  • Less fun than the tile boards

Best for

  • A desk monitor next to the scanner
  • Following several jobs at once

Nothing here changes the app. Pick a direction (or mix them, e.g. B's chevrons on A's palette) and it can be rolled into styles.css; the status colours, fonts and radii are all tokens already, so a switch is mostly a token swap plus a few accents.
